## Overview



Cherry MX ULP (Ultra Low Profile) switches are mechanical switches designed for extremely slim keyboard builds.
With a height of only 3.5 mm, they allow you to build mechanical keyboards that are significantly thinner than traditional MX or even other low-profile designs.

They are available in two variants: Tactile and Clicky.
Both feature a short 1.8 mm total travel and 0.8 mm pre-travel, making them quite different from the usual mechanical keyboard switches.

## Keycaps

Keep in mind that Cherry ULP switches are not compatible with regular Cherry MX or Kailh Choc Low Profile keycaps.
They use a proprietary mounting mechanism, so finding suitable keycaps can be a bit challenging.

## Soldering

Unlike most mechanical keyboard switches, Cherry MX ULP switches are designed for direct SMD mounting.
While there are Boards that allow to hand-solder these switches, the process is finicky and error-prone.
Therefore, soldering using a hot plate is recommended.

When soldering these switches, ensure there is not to much solder paste on the contact pads in the center of the switch.
This tends to flow inside the switch and solder the contact mechanism shut.
