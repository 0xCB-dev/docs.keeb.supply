## Assembly

You will receive your mnhTTn mostly assembled.
The last things you have to assemble is the battery and the keycaps.

### Battery

The battery has to be installed by removing the foil of the double-sided tape in the cutout on the bottom of the boards.
Place the batter onto of the sticky tape and give it a light press.
Then connect the two cables to the connector by pressing down on one of the levers and placing the cable into the hole.
Make sure to connect the red cable to the hole with the plus symbol and the black cable to the side with the minus symbol.

{{< callout context="note" title="ToDo" >}}
Trimming the cables to the right length before installing?
{{< /callout >}}


Then remove the foil of the double-sided tape on the battery cover and glue it ontop of the battery while ensuring all cables are tugged away under the cover.

### Keycaps

Ensure the alignment of each keycap is correct:
Check for the small nose on the bottom side of the keycap.
This nose, just like the spring inside the switch, is slightly off-center.
Ensure the nose is parallel to and on the same side as the spring.

!!! warn
    The keycaps have an orientation. Make sure to put them on right away.
    They are almost impossible to remove without breaking the switch.
    Ensure you have the right orientation.


{{< callout context="note" title="ToDo" >}}
Photo of keycap on its back beside a switch and a line showing the parallel alignment.
{{< /callout >}}

The keycaps are installed by aligning them with the key and pressing firmly from the top until they snap in place.
